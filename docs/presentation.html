Boutik

The Lightning till and credit notebook for Dakar's corner shops. Priced in FCFA. About 15 KB on a mid-range phone.

La caisse Lightning et le cahier de crédit du boutiquier.

The problem

The corner shop runs on cash, coins it doesn't have, and a paper notebook.

No change

A 2 000 note for a baguette ends in "I'll owe you", a candy instead of coins, or a lost sale.

Credit on paper

The cahier de crédit gets lost or disputed, and nobody can pay a tab from far away.

Wallets speak sats

The shopkeeper thinks in FCFA and needs one button, one QR and a clear "Payé ✓".

Primary user

One person behind the counter, a mid-range Android, intermittent 3G.

The solution

Four screens, all in FCFA.

🧾 Caisse

Tap products or type an amount. The server converts to sats, rounded up so the shop never gets less than the price.

📒 Ardoises

Debts stay in FCFA. The pay link creates a fresh invoice at today's rate when the customer opens it.

🎙️ Assistant

"2 pains, 3 cafés Touba et un sucre" → a draft cart. Claude when configured, offline French parser otherwise. It drafts; it never spends.

👛 Portefeuille

Balances in FCFA, receive on-chain, send behind PIN re-entry and a daily limit.

Working MVP · real LND nodes · regtest

From sentence to "Payé" in two taps.

Architecture · one command: docker compose up --build

Two real Lightning nodes. The till can't spend.

Optional egress from the app only: Claude API (assistant) and mempool.space / CoinGecko (BTC/EUR, converted at the fixed 655.957 XOF/EUR peg).

Security by design

Least privilege, from the macaroon to the log line.

  • The till can only create invoices. Tested: LND answers "permission denied" when the till's macaroon tries to pay. The app never sees admin.macaroon.
  • Every send needs the PIN again plus a network-prefix check, no zero-amount or expired invoices, a daily limit and a 1% fee cap.
  • The assistant only drafts. No tools, no macaroon. Output is schema-checked and totals are recomputed on the server.
  • No PII or capabilities in logs. Route templates only; invoices, hashes and pay-link tokens never written. Customer names never in invoices.
  • Hardened web layer. scrypt PIN + lockout, HttpOnly SameSite=Strict cookie, CSRF header, strict CSP, rate limits, read-only non-root container.
